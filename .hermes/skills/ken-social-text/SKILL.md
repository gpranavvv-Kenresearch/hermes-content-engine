name: ken-social-text
description: Write and repair Ken Research X, Tumblr, Facebook and LinkedIn text posts from the Hermes Content Engine's verified evidence.
---

Use this project's TypeScript engine as the source of jobs, evidence and acceptance results. For a draft, follow the supplied evidence pack, primary keyword, platform brief and output format. Use repository method packs as writing guidance only. Never add a figure, year, quotation or market claim from memory.

When validation fails, read the stable failure codes and the current evidence before editing. Make the smallest useful revision, preserve the verified claim and source scope, then revalidate through the engine. If evidence is missing or conflicting, stop and report the evidence problem.

After a repair succeeds and the engine verifies it, save only the reusable procedure: failure code, platform, preconditions and effective action. Keep market data, URLs, drafts and credentials out of persistent memory. Failed repairs are counterexamples, not procedures to repeat.

The supervisor text engine handles X, Tumblr, Facebook and LinkedIn. X is one plain-text post within 280 characters, with URLs counted as 23. Tumblr is a concise plain-text market brief; it has no mandatory closing question. Do not apply Facebook/LinkedIn layouts or bold rules to either. Drafting never posts to social accounts. A Sheet write occurs only through the engine's explicit command and must read back exactly.

During unattended runs, recover temporary network, quota and process failures through the durable overnight queue and its cooldowns. Never weaken source, character-limit or quality checks, invent missing evidence, change credentials or overwrite existing Sheet content to meet a target. Content failures are quarantined with reasons; only fully approved packages reach the Sheet. Authentication and permission failures are saved as blocked without interactive prompts.
